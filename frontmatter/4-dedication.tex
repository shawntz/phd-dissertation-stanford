\prefacesection{Dedication}

\vspace*{\fill}
\begin{center}
{\itshape
For my grandmother,\\[0.5em]
{\Large June Schwartz}\\[1.5em]
whose battle with Alzheimer's disease awakened in me\\
a desire to understand how the mind holds on to --- and\\
sometimes loses --- the experiences that make us who we are.\\[1.5em]
This work, and the questions it pursues, are for her.
}
\end{center}
